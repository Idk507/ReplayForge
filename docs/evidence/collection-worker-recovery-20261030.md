## Subsequent first-attempt fencing

The preceding restart concern is partially superseded: collection dispatch now requests only never-claimed jobs, and rechecks the acquired attempt under its active fence before POST. A previously claimed job is left for explicit reconciliation, even if the worker crashed before posting. This avoids repeated collection POSTs and consuming further attempts from the authenticated worker; it does not implement automatic recovery of uncertain or interrupted jobs. The existing 30-second lease can still expire while a first request runs, in which case existing fenced writes reject a stale lease. Operational visibility and an approved reconciliation procedure remain necessary.
